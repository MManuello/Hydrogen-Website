But first, What is Hydrogen?

Hydrogen is the most abundant element in the universe. It is a colorless, odorless, and highly flammable gas. Hydrogen is used in various industries, including the production of ammonia for fertilizers, refining petroleum, and as a fuel source for vehicles and power generation. It can be produced through several methods, including electrolysis of water and steam methane reforming.
